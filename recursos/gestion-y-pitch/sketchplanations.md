# Sketchplanations · Gestión y pitch

Catorce dibujos para los dos mazos de la clase. Se generan en Gemini y se guardan en `img/`
como webp (1600 px de ancho). Mismo estilo de las semanas 5 y 6 y de la clase de cronograma
y presupuesto: dibujo explicativo a mano, línea negra de plumón, figuras redondas y amables,
aguadas planas en teal, arena y un toque de rojo, mucho blanco, una sola idea, y como único
texto un título corto en español escrito a mano arriba.

Trucos: si Gemini mete más texto, agregar "absolutely no other writing"; si deletrea mal el
título, repetir la palabra entre comillas y pedir "spell it exactly, letter by letter"; si
pinta de más, cambiar la frase del color por "very sparse color, mostly black line on white
with two small color accents".

## Bloque común (va al inicio de cada prompt)

Generate an image. In the style of a Sketchplanations illustration: a hand-drawn explanatory sketch with clean, confident black fineliner outlines, slightly imperfect and friendly, drawn as if with a felt-tip pen on white paper. Simple, rounded, charming figures with minimal facial detail; objects simplified to their essential shapes. Light, flat watercolor-style color washes in a limited palette (soft teal, warm sand yellow, a touch of muted red for emphasis), applied loosely. Lots of white space, no shading gradients, no photorealism. Wide 16:9 composition. The only text allowed is one short hand-lettered Spanish title at the top of the picture, written exactly as given, spelled letter by letter; absolutely no other words, letters or numbers anywhere.

## Mazo 1 · Salir a conseguir

### 1 · dos-caminos.webp · título «DOS CAMINOS»
The picture: a rounded person carrying a rolled-up plan under one arm stands at a fork in a footpath. The left path leads to a small desk with a tall pile of papers, a pen and a mailbox with a slot, where a queue of four other small people wait holding envelopes. The right path leads to an open door where one person stands waiting with an open hand, ready for a handshake. Left path washed in sand, right path in teal, a touch of red on the mailbox flag.

### 2 · objetivo-comun.webp · título «OBJETIVO COMÚN»
The picture: a small river crosses the page. From the left bank, two young rounded people push half of a wooden footbridge; from the right bank, an older rounded person in a hat pushes the other half; the two halves are about to meet in the middle over the water. Behind each side, their own small house. Above the meeting point, a small sun. Bridge in sand, water in teal, a touch of red on the hat.

### 3 · no-solo-dinero.webp · título «NO SOLO DINERO»
The picture: five things arriving at a small building site from five directions, each carried by a rounded person: one brings a small bag with a coin; one brings a shovel over the shoulder; one drives a small borrowed pickup truck; one carries a sack of cement; one carries a basket of food. In the middle, the wooden frame of a small palm-roof shelter half built. Only the coin is red; the rest in teal and sand.

### 4 · la-ruta-de-una-gestion.webp · título «LA RUTA»
The picture: a winding footpath from left to right with five stations along it: first a rounded person looking through binoculars; second the same person knocking on a door; third the person holding out an open hand towards another person; fourth the person handing over a small card with a heart on it; fifth the person showing a photograph of a finished bench to the other person, who smiles. Small dotted footprints link the stations. Path in sand, door in teal, heart in red.

### 5 · es-una-competencia.webp · título «ES UNA COMPETENCIA»
The picture: on the right, a small wall with one little open window. From the left, about twenty paper airplanes fly towards it; most of them miss, bounce off the wall or lie crumpled on the ground; only three fly neatly through the window. Behind the window a rounded person with glasses catches one. At the far left a rounded person carefully folds a paper airplane on a table with a ruler. The three successful airplanes are teal, the rest plain white, one crumpled one red.

### 6 · van-dos.webp · título «VAN DOS»
The picture: an office desk seen from the side. Behind it sits a rounded official with a tie. In front of the desk, two young rounded people: one stands and points at a large paper plan spread on the desk showing a simple lake shore with three tiny huts; the other sits beside taking notes in a notebook, with a wristwatch clearly visible. On the wall, a simple round clock. Plan in teal and sand, a touch of red on the notebook.

### 7 · dar-las-gracias.webp · título «DAR LAS GRACIAS»
The picture: a small village hardware shop with an awning; the shopkeeper in an apron stands at the door. A young rounded person hands the shopkeeper a framed photograph showing a finished palm-roof shelter with a family sitting under it. Next to the shop door, a few sacks of cement stacked. The shopkeeper smiles broadly. Awning in teal, sacks in sand, picture frame in red.

## Mazo 2 · Dos minutos

### 8 · dos-minutos.webp · título «DOS MINUTOS»
The picture: a rounded young person stands talking, with one open hand, to a seated rounded person who leans forward listening. Between them, on a small table, a large hourglass with sand running. Behind the listener, a door that is beginning to open, with light coming through the gap. Hourglass sand in sand yellow, door in teal, a touch of red on the speaker's scarf.

### 9 · ponle-cara.webp · título «PONLE CARA»
The picture, in two halves: on the left, a rounded presenter points at a big bar chart on an easel while three listeners yawn or look at the ceiling. On the right, the same presenter holds up a simple portrait of one smiling old woman in a shawl, and the same three listeners lean forward, wide-eyed and attentive. The bar chart is plain grey line; the portrait has warm sand and teal washes and a red shawl.

### 10 · un-numero-que-se-ve.webp · título «UN NÚMERO QUE SE VE»
The picture, in two halves: on the left, a rounded person scratches their head in front of a huge plain iron weight block with a ring on top, question marks drawn as small curls above the head. On the right, the same person looks amazed at a small stream bed between two banks completely filled with tied garbage bags, with a tiny bridge over it. Weight block in plain line, garbage bags in sand with one in red, the banks in teal.

### 11 · palabras-de-humo.webp · título «PALABRAS DE HUMO»
The picture, in two halves: on the left, a rounded person speaks and from the mouth comes a big speech balloon made of drifting smoke clouds that dissolve into nothing; a listener squints trying to see something in the smoke. On the right, another rounded person speaks and the speech balloon contains a clear, solid little drawing of a palm-roof shelter with a bench; the listener nods and sketches the same shelter on a napkin. Smoke in light grey wash, shelter in teal and sand, pencil in red.

### 12 · cuatro-oidos.webp · título «CUATRO OÍDOS»
The picture: in the centre, one rounded young person holding a rolled plan. Around, four listeners, each with a very large ear turned towards the speaker: one in a tie holding a folder with a rubber stamp; one with glasses and a clipboard with check boxes; one shopkeeper in an apron beside a sack; and a small group of three villagers in hats sitting under a tree. A thin dotted line goes from the speaker to each ear. Tie in red, apron and tree in teal, sack and hats in sand.

### 13 · en-la-mano.webp · título «EN LA MANO»
The picture: a desk; on it, a rounded young person spreads a large photo-like drawing of a lake shore with three small palm huts sketched on it, and both the young person and an older official lean over it pointing with their fingers, interested. In the corner behind them, a projector sleeps on a shelf covered in a cobweb, with a small "z z" curl of sleep drawn as loops, not letters. The drawing on the desk in teal and sand, a red dot where the finger points.

### 14 · estoy-emocionado.webp · título «ESTOY EMOCIONADO»
The picture, in two halves: on the left, a rounded person stands stiff with knees trembling, small butterflies fluttering inside the outline of the belly and a small heart drawn beating fast. On the right, the same person stands open and smiling with arms slightly raised; the same butterflies now fly out and pull a kite upward on a string held in the hand; the same small heart still beats. Butterflies in teal, kite in sand, heart in red.
