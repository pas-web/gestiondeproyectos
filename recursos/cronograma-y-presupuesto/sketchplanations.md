# Sketchplanations · Cronograma y presupuesto

Trece dibujos para los tres mazos de la clase. Se generan en Gemini (Chrome de Eduardo) y
se guardan en `img/` como webp (1600 px de ancho). Estilo común, el mismo de las semanas 5 y 6:
dibujo explicativo a mano, línea negra de plumón, figuras redondas y amables, aguadas planas
en teal, arena y un toque de rojo, mucho blanco, una sola idea, y como único texto un título
corto en español escrito a mano arriba.

Trucos: si Gemini mete más texto, agregar "absolutely no other writing"; si deletrea mal el
título, repetir la palabra entre comillas y pedir "spell it exactly, letter by letter"; si
pinta de más, cambiar la frase del color por "very sparse color, mostly black line on white
with two small color accents".

## Bloque común (va al inicio de cada prompt)

In the style of a Sketchplanations illustration: a hand-drawn explanatory sketch with clean, confident black fineliner outlines, slightly imperfect and friendly, drawn as if with a felt-tip pen on white paper. Simple, rounded, charming figures with minimal facial detail; objects simplified to their essential shapes. Light, flat watercolor-style color washes in a limited palette (soft teal, warm sand yellow, a touch of muted red for emphasis), applied loosely. Lots of white space, no shading gradients, no photorealism. Wide 16:9 composition. The only text allowed is one short hand-lettered Spanish title at the top of the picture, written exactly as given; absolutely no other words, letters or numbers anywhere.

The picture: a big sheet of paper pinned to a wall showing a simple Gantt chart: six horizontal bars of different lengths stepping down from left to right like a staircase, in teal and sand washes, over a faint grid of vertical lines suggesting weeks; a small sun hangs above one column and a small rain cloud above another; a rounded person stands at the left pointing at one bar with a stick; one vertical line carries a tiny flag at the top.

### 2 · desmenuzar.webp · título «DESMENUZAR»
The picture: on the left, one big rounded boulder with a single label tag hanging from it; a curved arrow leads right to the same boulder split into four medium stones, each with its own tag; a second arrow leads further right to a neat row of small pebbles, each with a tiny tag, laid out in a line like beads. A small rounded person with a mallet stands between the boulder and the stones. Only the pebbles have color washes.

### 3 · la-casa-sin-paredes.webp · título «QUÉ ESPERA A QUÉ»
The picture: a rounded person with a paint roller and a bucket, cheerfully painting a wall that does not exist yet: only the foundation slab and four corner posts of a house are built, and the paint roller paints the air. Beside the person, a stack of bricks waits untouched. To the right, a small calendar page with a crossed-out day, and a tiny bell on a post. Teal paint, sand bricks, a touch of red on the roller.

### 4 · el-hito.webp · título «HITO»
The picture: a winding footpath drawn as two parallel wobbly lines crosses the page from left to right over gentle hills; along the path, at intervals, stand three old stone milestones, short rounded stones; one of them, in the middle, carries a small flag in muted red; a rounded person walks the path and has just reached that stone, looking at it; the sky holds one simple sun. Only the flag is red; the hills have a light teal wash.

### 5 · la-espera-y-la-holgura.webp · título «ESPERA Y HOLGURA»
The picture, in two halves separated by a faint vertical line: on the left, a freshly planted seedling in a mound of soil, then an hourglass, then the same plant taller with a rounded person crouching and counting its leaves with a finger; on the right, a short timeline bar with a small boat-shaped sled that can slide a little way along it before bumping into a fixed block, drawn with a double-headed arrow showing the slack. Seedling teal, hourglass sand, the fixed block red.

### 6 · la-joroba.webp · título «LA JOROBA»
The picture: a Gantt chart on a sheet of paper, with four weekly columns; the first three columns are almost empty, with one thin short bar each, and the last column is crammed with eight bars piled on top of each other so high that they bulge above the sheet into the shape of a camel's hump; a tiny camel stands beside the sheet looking at it; a rounded person with wide eyes holds the sheet. The hump is washed in muted red; the rest of the bars in teal.

### 7 · los-tres-relojes.webp · título «TRES RELOJES»
The picture: three simple round wall clocks in a row, each hanging from a nail. Inside the first clock face, instead of numbers, a small rain cloud at the top and a small sun at the bottom. Inside the second, a small money bag with a coin. Inside the third, a small group of three rounded people with a bell above them. Below the clocks, a single long horizontal bar like a schedule line, with three small arrows coming down from the three clocks to it. Clock faces in sand, cloud in teal, coin in red.

## Mazo 2 · El presupuesto

### 8 · el-arbol-de-35-pesos.webp · título «LO QUE CUESTA UN ÁRBOL»
The picture: an iceberg drawn as a simple shape on water. Above the waterline, on the tip, a single small seedling in a pot with a little price tag hanging from it. Below the waterline, much bigger, a jumble of simple objects: a shovel, a pickup truck, a watering can, a sack, a coil of wire fence, a roll of posts, a rounded person carrying a bucket, and a small calendar. The seedling is teal, the price tag red, the underwater objects in sand wash with faint blue water lines.

### 9 · de-la-actividad-a-los-pesos.webp · título «ACTIVIDAD A PESOS»
The picture, read left to right in three panels joined by arrows: first a sheet of paper with a short list of horizontal bars (a schedule); second, a simple shopping basket overflowing with a shovel, a plant, a sack and a coil of wire; third, a tidy stack of coins next to a small receipt with a few squiggled lines. A rounded person pushes the basket from the first panel to the second. Bars teal, basket sand, coins red.

### 10 · los-tres-hermanos.webp · título «TRES HERMANOS»
The picture: three rounded sibling figures standing side by side like a family portrait. The first, tallest, holds up a sheet of paper with a few squiggled lines and a small stamp. The second carries a heavy box on their back, bent forward, with a tiny pickup truck drawn beside their feet. The third, smallest, holds a small open umbrella over a tiny coin purse. Paper teal, box sand, umbrella red.

### 11 · dos-columnas.webp · título «DOS COLUMNAS»
The picture: an old two-pan balance scale. On the left pan, a neat stack of coins. On the right pan, a pile of things that are not money: a wooden pallet, a shovel, a pickup truck, a small group of three rounded people with hats, a watering hose. The two pans hang level. A rounded person stands beside the scale, reading a small notebook. Coins red, left pan teal, right pan sand.

### 12 · los-agujeros.webp · título «LOS AGUJEROS»
The picture: a simple cloth sack of coins held up by a rounded person; the sack has six small round holes, and from each hole a single coin drops out and falls toward the ground, drawing a dotted trail. On the ground below, a small pile of fallen coins. The sack is sand, the coins red, the person's shirt teal.

## Mazo 3 · Cobrar una capacitación

### 13 · el-iceberg-del-taller.webp · título «LO QUE NO SE VE DEL TALLER»
The picture: an iceberg on water. Above the waterline, a rounded person stands at a flip chart in front of three seated rounded people. Below the waterline, much bigger: the same person hunched at a desk with a lamp and papers, then driving a small car on a winding road, then carrying a box of supplies, then holding a stack of coins while a hand from the right takes a few away, and a calendar with many days crossed out. Flip chart teal, car sand, the hand taking coins red.

### 14 · tu-dia-de-trabajo.webp · título «TU DÍA»
The picture: a large monthly calendar page drawn as a grid of five rows by seven columns; most squares are empty or hold tiny icons (a car, a telephone, a desk, a bed), and only about twelve squares hold a small coin each; a rounded person sits on top of the calendar page dangling their legs, holding one coin up to the light. Coins red, the squares with icons in faint sand, the person teal.
